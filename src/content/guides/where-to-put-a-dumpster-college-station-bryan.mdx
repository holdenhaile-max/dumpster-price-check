## Bryan

The [City of Bryan right-of-way page](https://www.bryantx.gov/engineering-services/rightsofway/) lists the Right of Way Work Permit. Type A Special is $100. It applies to a contractor, a business, or a home owner. The bullets include placing dumpsters or PODs in the right-of-way, and "Placing a Dumpster (not on private property)."

Apply on the city's online permitting portal. The page says inquiries go to ROWpermit@bryantx.gov. The phone on the page is 979-209-5030. The office listed is Development Center, 200 E. 29th St., Bryan, TX 77803.

[Ordinance 2229, Section 106-172](https://www.bryantx.gov/media/uennwhjo/ordinance-2229-8_28_2017.pdf), sets the same $100 Type A fee. The ordinance names dumpsters in that fee, along with cranes, building materials, and tree trimming.

The right-of-way page also says Engineering Services can tell you the approximate distance from the curb line to the right-of-way line. Ask before you treat the strip by the street as your driveway.

The city does not haul a roll-off itself. The [permitted roll-off providers page](https://www.bryantx.gov/solid-waste/trash-collection-services/permitted-roll-off-providers/) says the service is separate from city service and the fee is paid to the provider. The page lists these names and phones:

- Brazos Valley Trash Valet and Recycling, 979-777-8968
- [BVR Waste and Recycling](/companies/bvr-waste-and-recycling), 979-260-0006
- Container Source Storage and Rolloff, 979-830-5350
- Maroon Dumpsters, 979-595-6446
- [Pronto Services](/companies/pronto-services), 979-665-4465
- [Texas Commercial Waste](/companies/texas-commercial-waste), 979-775-7171 on the city page
- [Texas Disposal Systems](/companies/texas-disposal-systems), 979-321-4123 on the city page

This site has no checked rental price for Brazos Valley Trash Valet and Recycling, Container Source Storage and Rolloff, or Maroon Dumpsters.

## College Station

The [franchise page](https://www.cstx.gov/business-development/franchise-agreements/) says debris hauling franchises allow a company that collects demolition and construction debris to use public streets, alleys, and public ways. That page does not list a resident permit fee.

[Section 26-10](https://library.municode.com/tx/college_station/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=SPAGEOR_CH26MIPROF_S26-6OPSTREAR) says no person shall, without permission of the City, store, dump, or dispose of litter, construction or earth materials, or other solid waste on a city road, street, right-of-way, thoroughfare, bridge, park, or other public place. The same section says doing that without prior permission is an offense.

[Section 40-402](https://library.municode.com/tx/college_station/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=SPAGEOR_CH40UT_ARTIVSOWACODI) says construction demolition waste shall be contained on-site. Rubbish that may blow must be kept in a container or a temporary holding pen. The city will not remove rock, construction demolition waste, or building materials from a remodel or a cleanup. The contractor, owner, or occupant removes that waste. The same section says the city will remove it only with roll-off containers, for a fee set in the code. Section 40-497 names a daily rental fee for a city-owned 20, 30, or 40 cubic yard roll-off, and a delivery and set-up fee, and points those dollar amounts to Section 2-117. The ordinance page that was read does not print those dollar amounts.

[Section 40-391](https://library.municode.com/tx/college_station/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=SPAGEOR_CH40UT_ARTIVSOWACODI) is the commercial collection rule. It says no person shall place a refuse container on a sidewalk or right-of-way unless the container is out for approved collection. It also says a container is not set out unless an all-weather route can hold the container and the service truck. The city is not responsible for damage to private drives and parking areas from those trucks. That section is written for commercial collection. It is not a printed homeowner driveway permit.

The [driveway and curb cut page](https://www.cstx.gov/living-here/building-and-renovating/building-permits/driveway-and-curb-cuts/) is about building or changing a driveway. It does not mention a dumpster. This guide does not treat that permit as a dumpster permit.

## What the company pages say about the spot

[BCS Junk Removal](/companies/bcs-junk-removal) says the 15-yard dumpster fits in one parking space. The rental is $380 for 3 days or $420 for 7 days, plus a $60 per ton dump fee. Included tons are not listed. The [live load page](https://bcsjunkremoval.com/live-loads) says some HOAs will not allow a dumpster on the lot. A live load is $325 for 45 minutes or $385 for 90 minutes. The company says no city permit is needed because the dumpster is on site only for that short time. The city pages above do not say a short live load is exempt.

[Pronto Services](/companies/pronto-services) says the 20-yard fits most residential driveways. That page does not mention a permit, and it does not publish a dollar rental price.

The other company pages used for the price table do not state a driveway rule or a street permit. This guide does not fill one in.
