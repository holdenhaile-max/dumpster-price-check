## The short rentals with a dollar price

A weekend is 1 to 3 days. The table above keeps a row only when the company page states a rental of 3 days or less and states a dollar price. These pages were read on Nov 26, 2026.

Aggieland Dumpster Rentals lists three short packages. They are dump trailers. The yard size is not exact.

- 1 day: $300 plus tax. 2 tons included.
- 2 days: $350 plus tax. 2 tons included.
- 3 days: $400 plus tax. 2 tons included.

BCS Junk Removal lists one short package. It is a 15-yard roll-off.

- 3 days: $380. The dump fee is $60 per ton. Included tons are not listed.

BCS also lists 7 days at $420. That is not a 1 to 3 day rental, so it is not in the table above. The [7-day price guide](/guides/cheapest-7-day-dumpster-brazos-valley) compares the longer rentals.

## What is included

Aggieland's rate includes 2 tons and delivery within 20 miles of 77845. Extra weight is $50 per ton. A delivery farther than 20 miles from the shop in College Station is $50. A mid-rental swap is $125. The homepage price is plus tax. The tax dollar amount is not on the page. The page does not list a price for a day past the 1-day, 2-day, 3-day, or 7-day package.

BCS lists the 3-day base price and a dump fee of $60 per ton. The dumpster page does not say how many tons are included. This site does not treat that fee as starting after a free ton. Tax, a delivery fee, and an extra day fee are not on the page. The terms page lists the same 15-yard dumpster at $380 for 3 days and $420 for 7 days, with the same $60 per ton dump fee.

Every company's tons, delivery, per-ton fee, and extra day are in the [what's included guide](/guides/whats-included-dumpster-rental).

## Prices that are not a 1 to 3 day rental

Waste Falcon's booking pages list dollar prices. None of them state 1, 2, or 3 days. The 10-yard page is $365 and does not list the days. The 15-yard page is $380 and does not list the days. The 15-yard swap is $380 and does not list the days. The 20-yard page says up to 7 days. The 30-yard page says up to 10 days. The marketing page says a 7-day rental. Those are real prices. They are not in the table above.

Rocking H Dumpsters lists up to 10 days, not 1, 2, or 3 days. The 10-yard is $295, the 20-yard is $395, the 30-yard is $495, and the 40-yard is $595. Those prices are not in the table above.
