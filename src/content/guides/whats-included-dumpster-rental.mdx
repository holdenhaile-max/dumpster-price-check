## What the pages actually include

The rental price is not the whole bill. Per ton means the fee for each ton past the included weight. The table lists every size these companies publish. A cell says Not listed when that company's page does not state the number.

- Aggieland Dumpster Rentals includes 2 tons, then $50 per ton. Delivery is included within 20 miles of 77845, and $50 if farther. The packages are 1, 2, 3, or 7 days. A fee for a day past the package is not listed. The yard size is not exact.
- BCS Junk Removal lists 3 days or 7 days and a dump fee of $60 per ton. Included tons, a delivery fee, and an extra day fee are not listed.
- Waste Falcon's 20-yard booking page includes 7 days and 0 tons, then $42 per ton, and $8 a day after day 10. It does not say what days 8, 9, and 10 cost. The 30-yard booking page includes 10 days and 0 tons, with the same per-ton and extra-day fees. The marketing page says $45 per ton and $8 a day after day 7. Those two rows use the booking page.
- Waste Falcon's 10-yard, 15-yard, and 15-yard swap booking pages do not list days, tons, a per-ton fee, an extra day fee, or a delivery fee. Those fees are not copied from the other Waste Falcon pages.
- Pronto Services lists weight limits of 4 tons (8,000 lbs), 5 tons (10,000 lbs), and 6 tons (12,000 lbs). The page says rentals run 7 to 10 days with daily extensions. It does not publish one included day count, a per-ton fee, or an extra day price, so those cells say Not listed.
- Bin There Dump That Aggieland says a typical rental is 7 days. Included tons, a per-ton fee, an extra day fee, and a delivery dollar amount are not listed.
- CTI Response, Texas Disposal Systems, Texas Commercial Waste, and BVR Waste and Recycling do not publish a number for days, tons, delivery, per ton, or an extra day.

The Checked date on each row is the day that company's page was read. The [1 to 3 day prices](/guides/1-3-day-weekend-dumpster-prices) and the [7-day prices](/guides/cheapest-7-day-dumpster-brazos-valley) compare the base rental. What each company says can go in the dumpster is in the [what can go in guide](/guides/what-can-you-put-in-a-dumpster).
