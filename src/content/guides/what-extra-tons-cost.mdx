## When the page states the included tons

Aggieland Dumpster Rentals includes 2 tons, then $50 per ton. At 1 ton and at 2 tons the ton fee is $0, so the total is the base price plus tax. At 3 tons the fee is $50. At 4 tons the fee is $100. A 1-day rental is $300 plus tax at 1 or 2 tons, and $350 plus tax at 3 tons. Delivery inside 20 miles of 77845 is included. The tax dollar amount is not published. The yard size is not exact.

Waste Falcon's 20-yard booking page includes 0 tons, then $42 per ton, for up to 7 days, at $425. One ton is $42, and $425 plus $42 is $467. Two tons is $84, and $425 plus $84 is $509. Three tons is $126, and $425 plus $126 is $551. Four tons is $168, and $425 plus $168 is $593.

The 30-yard booking page is $475 for up to 10 days, with 0 tons included and the same $42 per ton. Two tons is $84, and $475 plus $84 is $559. Four tons is $168, and $475 plus $168 is $643.

The marketing page says $45 per ton. At 2 tons that is $90. $425 plus $90 is $515. $475 plus $90 is $565. The table uses the booking page. Tax on the Waste Falcon rows is not listed.

## When included tons are not stated

BCS Junk Removal lists a dump fee of $60 per ton and does not state how many tons are included. The next table bills every ton. That is addition from the published rate. It is not a statement that the allowance is 0 tons.

<OverageTable mode="every-ton" caption="BCS totals if every ton is billed at $60. Included tons are not listed." />

A 7-day BCS rental at 2 tons is $420 plus $120, which is $540. A 3-day rental at 2 tons is $380 plus $120, which is $500. At 4 tons the 7-day line is $420 plus $240, which is $660. Tax is not listed.

## A price with no per-ton fee on that page

These booking pages list a dollar price and do not list a per-ton fee. The fee from another Waste Falcon page is not copied here.
