faqs:
  - question: What does Not published mean?
    answer: That company's own page did not state it. Not published is not a yes. Confirm with the company before you load the dumpster.
  - question: Which companies publish a tire rule?
    answer: Aggieland's FAQ says no item is completely prohibited, and tires can add a fee. That fee amount is not published. BCS terms say tires are taken for $10 each. Waste Falcon's homepage FAQ says tires are not allowed. The homepage cost answer and the pricing page list tires at $10 each. Those statements disagree. Pronto Services and Texas Disposal Systems list tires with the items they do not take. Bin There Dump That Aggieland, BVR Waste and Recycling, CTI Response, and Texas Commercial Waste do not publish a tire rule.
  - question: What about paint, batteries, and appliances with Freon?
    answer: Hazardous waste, paint, tires, batteries, and appliances with Freon are commonly kept out of a dumpster. That is general guidance, not a rule from every company here. Waste Falcon's homepage FAQ says paint, chemicals, batteries, and tires are not allowed, and it lists appliances as allowed. The homepage cost answer and the pricing page list Freon units at $25 each. Texas Disposal Systems prohibits paint, batteries, and tires, and it accepts appliances with Freon removed. It does not publish a Freon fee. Aggieland says Freon units can add a fee and does not publish the amount. It does not name paint or batteries. Pronto says appliances are typically accepted, sometimes with an extra disposal fee, and that fee is not published. Pronto does not name paint or batteries. BVR's commercial roll-off FAQ says asbestos, batteries, and flammable liquids are not allowed, and it says concrete, asphalt, wood, and scrap metal can go in. It does not name paint, tires, or Freon. Bin There Dump That Aggieland, CTI Response, Texas Commercial Waste, and BCS Junk Removal do not name paint, batteries, or Freon.
  - question: Does a fee mean the item is allowed?
    answer: Only when that company's page says so. Aggieland says those items are not completely prohibited and that a fee can apply. BCS terms say tires are taken for $10. Pronto says some items are typically accepted and that an extra fee can apply, and it does not publish the amount. Waste Falcon lists a tire fee on the homepage cost answer and the pricing page, and a tire ban in the homepage FAQ. Read both before you load tires there.
