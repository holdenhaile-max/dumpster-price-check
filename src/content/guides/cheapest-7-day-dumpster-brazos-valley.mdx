## Read the tons before you pick the lowest price

The lowest base price in the table is $420 from BCS Junk Removal. That is not a full bill. BCS does not state included tons and bills $60 per ton. Waste Falcon's 20-yard booking page is $425 with 0 tons included and $42 per ton. Aggieland is $450 plus tax and includes 2 tons.

For 2 tons, using only those published rates:

- Aggieland, 7 days: $450 plus tax. The 2 tons are included, so the ton fee does not add if you stay at 2 tons. Delivery is included within 20 miles of 77845.
- Waste Falcon 20-yard booking page: $425 plus $42 per ton. Two tons is $84. $425 plus $84 is $509. Tax is not listed. The marketing page's $45 per ton would make two tons $90, and $425 plus $90 is $515. This row does not use $45.
- BCS Junk Removal, 7 days: $420 plus $60 per ton. Two tons is $120. $420 plus $120 is $540. Tax is not listed. This math treats every ton as billed, because the page does not state an allowance.

These lines are addition. They are not quotes. Your debris weight can be different, and a fee that says Not listed can still show up on the invoice.
