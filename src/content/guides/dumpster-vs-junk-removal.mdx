## Which one fits you

A dumpster rental leaves the container for the days on that row. Junk removal is the company hauling the junk. Read the row before you treat two prices as the same job.

BCS Junk Removal publishes both. The 15-yard dumpster is $380 for 3 days and $420 for 7 days. The dump fee is $60 per ton, and included tons are not listed. The full 15-yard haul-off is $650. That page says they fill the container for one flat price. A live load is $325 for 45 minutes or $385 for 90 minutes. The driver brings a 15-yard container and waits. You load it. Up to 1 ton is permitted, then $60 per ton. The live load page says this suits a lot where a dumpster is not allowed to sit.

One item at BCS starts at $125, and that price includes one item. Furniture and appliances are listed at $20 to $30 each on top of that minimum. The same page also says the cost of extra items is set when you get a quote, and that online prices can change with the job. Canceling with less than 24 hours notice costs half of the appointment total. No dollar amount is listed for that fee.

ME3 Junk Solutions publishes prices for Bryan and College Station. The minimum is $150. Full service is $225 for 4 cubic yards, $450 for 8, and $650 for 12. A 16 to 16.5 cubic yard load is listed as a $900 target, and the page also says that full load targets about $900. Those full service prices are for standard household material. The page says dense material is quoted on its own, and that heavy or specialty loads can exceed $900 to $1,200+. There is no trip charge within about 40 miles of Bryan and College Station.

979 Junk Removal is based in College Station and lists Bryan. The item prices are ranges. A mattress is $40 to $60. A large couch is $75 to $100. A refrigerator is $75 to $100. The page says they do the carrying, hauling, and disposal, and that the text quote is the price you pay.

Aggieland Dumpster Rentals lists 1 day at $300 plus tax, with 2 tons included. The yard size is not exact. Waste Falcon lists dollar prices on its booking pages. Some of those pages do not list the days. The [what's included guide](/guides/whats-included-dumpster-rental) has tons, delivery, and extra fees for every dumpster company.

Companies with no dollar dumpster price stay on the [College Station](/cities/college-station) and [Bryan](/cities/bryan) pages as Call for quote. They are not in the table above.
