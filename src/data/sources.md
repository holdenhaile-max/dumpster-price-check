# BCS dumpster price sources

Checked 2026-11-19 from each company's own public pages. No businesses were contacted, and no forms were submitted. Third-party listings were used only to discover company URLs, never as price sources.

The price files in `src/data/companies/` are the source of truth for the site. This note records what each company did and did not publish.

## Companies with published dumpster rental prices

### Aggieland Dumpster Rentals

- URLs: https://aggielanddumpsters.com/ and https://aggielanddumpsters.com/faqs/
- Published: 1, 2, 3, and 7 day packages at $300, $350, $400, and $450 plus tax. 2 tons included. $50 per ton after that. Delivery included within 20 miles of 77845. $50 if farther. $125 mid-rental swap.
- Not published: exact yardage (FAQ says the trailers average just under 20 yards), extra day rate, deposit, tax amount, fees for tires, Freon, and chemicals.

### BCS Junk Removal

- URL: https://bcsjunkremoval.com/dumpster-rental-service
- Published: 15-yard dumpster, 3 days $380 and 7 days $420. Dump fee $60 per ton.
- Not published: included tons on the rental, extra day fee, delivery fee, deposit, tax.
- Junk removal prices are stored on the company file and are not mixed into the dumpster price sort.

### Waste Falcon

- Booking pages list: 10-yard $365, 15-yard $380, 15-yard swap $380, 20-yard $425, 30-yard $475.
- 20-yard booking page: up to 7 days, 0 tons included, $42 per ton, $8 a day after day 10.
- 30-yard booking page: up to 10 days, 0 tons included, $42 per ton, $8 a day after day 10.
- Marketing page: 7 days, $45 per ton, $8 a day after day 7.
- The site shows the booking page figures. Rows that display the disputed fees are marked sources disagree.
- 10-yard, 15-yard, and swap booking pages do not list days, tons, or a per-ton fee. Those cells stay Not listed.
